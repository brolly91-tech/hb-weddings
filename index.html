About me

I’m Hannah Brolly, the face behind Hannah Brolly Weddings. I chose to explore this industry as I have a passion for organising and planning. With a Type-A personality I felt like I found my niche in helping couples pull together their dream wedding without stress and fuss.

With a creative personality, my strengths lie within the details, minor or major. I love to deep dive into the finer details and find the sentimentality that makes each wedding unique. With my own wedding, I found this part so enjoyable as I felt like a story was being told throughout the day. I want to help all my couples experience their story on their big day and look back with memories to remember forever.

"Step Under The Brolly"

I want to make each couple feel like they are planning their wedding with a friend, that joy and excitement is felt throughout each planning process. My mission is to make this chapter of your life unforgettable.

Tell me your plans

I am there to assist you and your bridal party on the morning of the wedding. This includes dress preparation together with making sure all the intricate details are accounted for on the morning. Services included are:

  • Dress steaming for bride and bridal party.
  • Dress service for bride and bridal party.
  • Answering any queries / calls that may pop up on the day.
  • Liaising with vendors the morning off.
  • Timeline management.
  • Bridal party coordination.

4–6 weeks out from your wedding, I take over the remaining planning process to allow you and your partner to relax and fully enjoy the lead-up to the wedding.

  • Handover of all final details.
  • Communicating with suppliers.
  • Confirming final details with suppliers.
  • Full wedding day management.
  • Morning service included.

You have just got engaged and do not know where to start? Well, step under the Brolly. I will be there throughout the whole process to help you plan your dream wedding. This is a bespoke package for each couple.

  • One on one planning service.
  • Budget management.
  • Ongoing communication with suppliers.
  • Timeline management.
  • Vendor coordination.
  • Venue Coordinator.
  • Morning Service included.
